Zen Mode privacy policy

Effective 6 November 2026

In short: your places and your activity log stay on your phone. ShipKnot has no server, no accounts and no analytics, so it never receives them.

Zen Mode is an Android app by ShipKnot that silences your phone at places you choose. This policy explains what the app handles, why, and where any of it goes.

What stays on your phone

  • Your places: each place's name, location, radius, mode (Do Not Disturb, Silent or Vibrate), Wi-Fi network names, schedule and call exceptions.
  • Activity log: when you arrived at or left a place and when the sound changed, with what caused it. Lines older than one year are deleted automatically.
  • App state: whether you are at a place, and the ringer setting to restore when you leave.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. It is not uploaded anywhere, not included in Android backups and not copied to a new phone.

Permissions

  • Location, including "Allow all the time": to notice when you arrive at or leave a saved place, even while the app is closed. Zen Mode does not keep a history of where you go.
  • Wi-Fi network name: to recognize a place by its Wi-Fi. Android only gives apps the network name together with location access.
  • Do Not Disturb access: to silence your phone at a place and bring the sound back when you leave.
  • Notifications: to tell you when something stops your places from working, such as location being off.
  • Run at startup: to set your places up again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: to load the map and search for addresses while you choose a place.

Services Zen Mode uses

A few features rely on services run by others. Each receives only what its feature needs:

  • Google Play services (location): Zen Mode registers each place's location and radius with Google Play services on your phone, which watches for arrivals and departures, and asks it for your current location when you choose a place on the map. Google handles this under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Address search: when you search for a place, the words you type go to Android's address lookup, which on most phones is provided by Google.
  • Map: when you view the map, your phone downloads map images from OpenStreetMap's servers, which see your IP address and the area shown. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

What Zen Mode does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking, and no crash-reporting tools.
  • No selling or sharing of your information. ShipKnot never receives your places, Wi-Fi names or activity.

If you allow Android to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may give ShipKnot crash and performance reports about the app. These reports do not include your places or your activity log.

Sharing your activity log

Your activity log leaves your phone only if you tap Share log on the Activity screen. You choose the app and the person to send it to, for example when reporting a problem to ShipKnot. The file contains your place names, the time of each change and what caused it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Places stay until you delete them in the app, and activity log lines are deleted after one year. Uninstalling Zen Mode, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored. ShipKnot holds no copy. If you have emailed ShipKnot or sent a log, ask and it will be deleted.

Children

Zen Mode is not directed at children under 13, and ShipKnot does not collect personal information through the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
